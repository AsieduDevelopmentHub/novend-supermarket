# Spacing System

Version: 1.0.0

Use the Tailwind spacing scale. Prefer 4 / 6 / 8 / 12 / 16.

## Page

- App main: `px-4 py-6 lg:px-8`
- Section gaps: `gap-6`
- Card padding: `p-4` or `p-6`
- Form field stacks: `space-y-4`
- Compact POS gutters: `gap-3` / `p-3`

Whitespace is intentional. Do not pack cashier screens until they feel cramped;
do not pad admin tables until they feel sparse.
